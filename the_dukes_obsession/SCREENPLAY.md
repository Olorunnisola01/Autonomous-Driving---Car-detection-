# THE DUKE'S OBSESSION

*A Gothic Dark Romance*

## Production Overview

**Format:** 25-scene storyboard (5 acts × 5 scenes × 10 steps)
**Total Images:** 258 (8 character refs + 250 scene steps)
**Genre:** Gothic dark romance; possessive, slow-burn, morally grey
**Setting:** Blackthorn Hall, a decaying Regency-era manor; winter

## Logline

When an impoverished bookbinder's daughter is summoned to catalog the library of a reclusive, widowed Duke rumored to have killed his first wife, she discovers her name already written in his journals from ten years before she was born — and a contract offering her family's debts cleared in exchange for one winter as his Duchess in name only.

**Theme:** Love that feels like being claimed by winter — terrifying, consuming, and finally, chosen.

## Visual Style Guide

Gothic dark romance; Regency England manor in deep winter; candlelight, velvet, frost, decaying opulence; saturated deep crimson, charcoal, ivory, gold; tactile skin, breath fog, 16:9 widescreen; no text, no logos, no distorted hands.

## Character Bible (8 Characters)

### @ELARA_FINCH — Elara Finch
Age 23; bookbinder's daughter, protagonist.
Pale ivory skin with freckles, hazel eyes (green-gold), auburn hair in messy coil, ink-stained fingertips.
**Wardrobe:** forest-green wool dress, cream linen apron, leather bookbinder's gloves at belt, worn ankle boots.
**Object:** small brass thimble on chain around neck.

### @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN — Duke Silas Blackthorn
Age 32; reclusive widowed Duke, male lead.
Tall, broad-shouldered, sharp aristocratic features, dark charcoal eyes, black hair slightly too long, scar through left eyebrow.
**Wardrobe:** black wool coat with silver buttons, ivory cravat, dark grey waistcoat, black leather gloves.
**Object:** Blackthorn signet ring on right hand.

### @MRS_VARMA — Mrs. Varma
Age 58; Duke's housekeeper.
Dark olive skin, silver-streaked black hair pulled tight, knowing eyes.
**Wardrobe:** black silk dress with high collar, white apron with lace trim, heavy iron key ring at waist.
**Object:** iron house key on black ribbon.

### @JULIAN_FOX — Julian Fox
Age 30; Duke's charming, predatory cousin; antagonist.
Sandy blond hair, ice-blue eyes, lazy smile, elegant cruelty.
**Wardrobe:** burgundy velvet coat with gold embroidery, cream silk shirt, tan riding breeches, polished brown boots.
**Object:** gold-tipped riding crop.

### @LADY_MARGARET — Lady Margaret
Deceased, appeared 25 in portraits; the Duke's first wife.
Raven-black hair, porcelain skin, eyes that hold a warning.
**Portrait wardrobe:** white satin gown, pearl choker, dark emerald pendant.
**Object:** emerald pendant.

### @THOMAS_FINCH — Thomas Finch
Age 52; Elara's father, ruined bookbinder.
Thin, stooped, grey-streaked auburn hair, trembling hands, haunted eyes.
**Wardrobe:** threadbare brown wool coat, stained linen shirt, patched trousers, leather apron.
**Object:** cracked bookbinding press.

### @LUCY — Lucy
Age 19; maid at Blackthorn Hall; Elara's ally.
Round-faced, rosy cheeks, auburn-brown hair in twin braids.
**Wardrobe:** grey wool maid's dress, white cap and apron.
**Object:** tin of stolen candles.

### @FATHER_BENEDICT — Father Benedict
Age 65; village vicar.
Tall, thin, kind weathered features, white beard, watery blue eyes.
**Wardrobe:** black cassock with white clerical collar, heavy wool scarf.
**Object:** leather-bound prayer book.

## The 25 Scenes

### ACT I — INHERITANCE (S01-S05)

**S01 — The Debt That Breathes** (FINCH_BINDERY, evening)
Thomas hunches over cracked binding press, hands shaking. Elara stitches a Bible's spine. Creditor notices paper the walls. Elara says they can sell the Chaucer. Thomas says Mother loved that one. Elara: "Then it is worth more alive than dead."

**S02 — The Summons Arrives** (FINCH_BINDERY, morning)
Black-sealed letter slides under the door. Blackthorn crest in wax. Commands the daughter of Thomas Finch to catalog the late Duchess's library at Blackthorn Hall. Payment enough to clear the debt. Father's eyes widen with recognition — and terror.

**S03 — The Road to Blackthorn** (WINTER_ROAD, day)
Elara walks alone along frost-bitten road with leather satchel. Skeletal hedgerows. Breath in white clouds. Milestone carved with thorned rose. She knows she is close. Every story about that house ends the same way. She does not turn back.

**S04 — The Gates of Blackthorn Hall** (BLACKTHORN_GATES, dusk)
Iron gates taller than a man, twisted into thorned vines. Mrs. Varma waits in the gateway like a sentinel. Does not smile. Measures Elara with one look. "You will sleep in the east wing. Do not use the upper corridor."

**S05 — The First Glimpse of Him** (BLACKTHORN_FOYER, night)
Cavernous foyer with candelabras. Elara removes ink-stained gloves. At top of staircase, half in shadow, stands Duke Silas Blackthorn. He watches her patiently, then turns and disappears. Elara whispers: "He was not surprised to see me."

### ACT II — OBSESSION (S06-S10)

**S06 — The Library That Remembered** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, morning)
Two stories of shelves bowing under centuries of books. Dust motes in slanting winter light. Fireplace large enough to stand in holds cold ashes. Elara touches a spine and feels recognition. Mrs. Varma: "She arranged it by feeling, not subject."

**S07 — The Journals with Her Name** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, afternoon)
Elara catalogues cracked leather journals. Opens one dated thirteen years ago. Finds her own name — ELARA FINCH — written in elegant copperplate in a margin note. The date is ten years before she was born. He knew before she existed.

**S08 — The Duke Watches** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, evening)
Candlelight. Elara works late, unaware of the figure in the doorway's shadow. He watches her lift a page with a bone folder. She senses him — in the air pressure, in the stillness. When she turns, he is already gone. Only scent of cold wool and smoke remains.

**S09 — The Gallery of Dead Wives** (BLACKTHORN_GALLERY, night)
Lucy shows Elara the house by candlelight. Long corridor of portraits. Stop before Lady Margaret — white satin, pearl choker, emerald pendant, dark knowing eyes. Lucy: "They say he locked himself in here for a year after she died." "How did she die?" "That is the question no one answers twice the same way."

**S10 — The First Touch** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, late night)
Elara falls asleep at the reading desk. Duke enters silently. His gloved hand brushes a stray auburn curl from her forehead. Finger grazes her temple. She stirs. Eyes meet across the smallest distance. His voice barely audible: "You are exactly as I wrote you."

### ACT III — THE BARGAIN (S11-S15)

**S11 — The Contract on the Desk** (BLACKTHORN_STUDY, morning)
Black oak desk. Single document. Duke stands behind it, signet ring catching firelight. All Finch debts cleared in exchange for one winter as Duchess. In name, in public, in residence. No conjugal obligation. She may leave at spring thaw. "Why me?" "Because you were always the answer."

**S12 — The Fake Engagement** (BLACKTHORN_DINING_HALL, evening)
Long table set for two, though house holds twenty. Mrs. Varma adjusts Elara's collar. Lucy peeks from doorway. Duke: "You will call me Silas in private. In public, Your Grace. Allow no one to see you smile first." "And if I smile first?" "Then I will spend the evening making it worth the scandal."

**S13 — The Jealousy at the Hunt Ball** (BLACKTHORN_BALLROOM, night)
Ballroom glittering. Julian in burgundy velvet claims Elara for a dance. Hand at her waist. Duke watches from across room, brandy untouched, knuckles white on glass. Julian: "They say my cousin chose a bookbinder." "Then why are you trembling?" Duke crosses the room, takes her hand from Julian's waist.

**S14 — The Secret of the First Wife** (BLACKTHORN_STUDY, late night)
Elara finds locked drawer, forces it open with bone folder. Letters from Lady Margaret's brother describing slow poisoning. Doctor's note that Duke refused autopsy. She confronts him. "Did you kill her?" "She asked me to let her go. I held on too tightly." "And me?" "You are the only thing I have ever been afraid to hold."

**S15 — The Agreement** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, dawn)
Winter light turns the library gold. Elara by window, contract in hands. Duke by fireplace. She has found the escape clause: may leave at spring, debts cleared, no conditions. "I will stay the winter. Not because of the debt. Because I need to know if what you wrote is true — or if I am just the woman who looked enough like a ghost to fill the space." "You were never a ghost. You were the only living thing I could imagine in this house."

### ACT IV — RUIN (S16-S20)

**S16 — The Betrayal** (BLACKTHORN_STUDY, afternoon)
Julian finds Elara alone. Reveals Duke's journals contain entries about ownership, not love. He has been watching her since childhood. Purchased father's debt three years ago. "You are not a bride, my dear. You are a collection." Duke enters. Does not deny it.

**S17 — The Fire in the East Wing** (BLACKTHORN_EAST_WING, night)
Elara has packed her satchel. Smell of smoke. East wing fills with orange light. Lucy screams. Mrs. Varma presses a key into Elara's hand — servants' exit. "Go. Now. Do not look for him." Elara runs through smoke, does not look at the burning gallery where Lady Margaret's portrait turns to ash.

**S18 — She Tries to Flee** (BLACKTHORN_GROUNDS, pre-dawn)
Elara stumbles through snow, green dress stained with soot, brass thimble swinging. Frost bites through boots. Walks a mile then realizes she's been walking in a circle — her own footprints ahead of her. Alone. Lost. Furious. "I will not be the ghost. I will not be the collection. I will not be the thing he wrote."

**S19 — He Lets Her Go** (BLACKTHORN_GATES, dawn)
Iron gates at first light. Duke stands on the other side with saddled horse, traveling cloak, letter of transit. Has not come to stop her. Has come to arm her. "I bought the debt because I could not bear the thought of you starving. I did not arrange you. I waited for you. There is a difference." Opens the gate. "Go. If you return, it must be because you chose to."

**S20 — The Distance Between** (VILLAGE_INN, day)
Elara sits by fire in village inn, wrapped in his traveling cloak. Father Benedict brings tea. "He came to me after Margaret died and said, 'Tell me how to stop loving what I cannot save.' I told him there is no stopping. Only choosing." "What did he choose?" "He chose to wait. For ten years. That is not obsession, child. That is the only patience I have ever seen that deserved to be called love."

### ACT V — DEVOTION (S21-S25)

**S21 — The Return on Her Own Terms** (BLACKTHORN_GATES, dusk)
Elara returns. Not in his cloak — she returned it. Own forest-green dress, clean but worn. No contract. Only herself. Mrs. Varma opens the gate. Duke in gravel drive, snow in his hair. "I did not come back for the contract." "I know." "I came back because the alternative was a lifetime of listening for your footsteps and pretending I did not miss them."

**S22 — The Consummation** (BLACKTHORN_CHAMBERS, night)
Private chambers. High windows black with winter night. Fire low and amber. No contract between them now. He removes his gloves slowly. Bare hands take her face as if she were a text he had been trying to read for a decade. "Say my name." "Silas." He closes his eyes. First time he heard his own name spoken like a prayer.

**S23 — The Public Claiming** (BLACKTHORN_BALLROOM, night)
Second ball — this one Elara's choice. Enters on Duke's arm as chosen Duchess. Julian watches, smile sharp. Mrs. Varma smiles for the first time. Lucy mouths "finally." Duke leads her to center of floor. "Let them stare. Let them write it down. They will never be able to say I did not ask." "You asked every day. I simply could not hear it until I was ready to answer."

**S24 — The Marriage** (VILLAGE_CHURCH, morning)
Small village church, snow on windowsills. Thomas walks his daughter down the aisle, hands steadier than in years. Lucy holds flowers. Mrs. Varma as witness. Father Benedict's voice cracks on "join." Signet ring warm against her finger, beside brass thimble chain, beside ink that will never wash out. "I do." "I always have."

**S25 — The True Duchess** (BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY, winter morning)
Years later. Library alive now — curtains open, fire lit, books scattered. Elara in reading chair with child on knee, pointing at gold lettering. Duke behind her, hand on chair's back. Room smells of old paper and beeswax. Thorned rose crest on armrest now looks like welcome, not warning. "And the Duke kept his word. Every word. Every winter. Every one." "Not every word. I told you once I would survive without you." "That was the only lie you ever told." The child laughs. The fire crackles. FADE OUT.
