# THE DUKE'S OBSESSION

*A Gothic Dark Romance*

## Production overview

**Format:** Feature screenplay / 25-scene storyboard
**Genre & tone:** Gothic dark romance; possessive, slow-burn, morally grey; an obsessive love story wrapped in velvet and frost.
**Setting:** Blackthorn Hall, a decaying Regency-era manor in the English countryside; winter.
**Logline:** When an impoverished bookbinder's daughter is summoned to catalog the library of a reclusive, widowed Duke rumored to have killed his first wife, she discovers her name already written in his journals from ten years before she was born — and a contract offering her family's debts cleared in exchange for one winter as his Duchess in name only.
**Theme:** Love that feels like being claimed by winter — terrifying, consuming, and finally, chosen.

## Visual style guide

Gothic dark romance; Regency England manor in deep winter; candlelight, velvet, frost, decaying opulence; saturated deep crimson, charcoal, ivory, gold; tactile skin, breath fog, 16:9 widescreen composition; subtle film grain; expressive moody lighting. No written words, no logos, no watermark, no distorted hands, no duplicate people.

### Continuity rules

- Keep each principal in the exact signature wardrobe and signature object specified below in every present-day frame.
- Reuse each location description verbatim in image prompts.
- Favour intimate scale images, real working hands, tactile leather, candlelight, frost on glass, velvet, gold leaf, ink, old paper.
- Maintain 16:9 landscape compositions for every scene image and avoid readable text, logos, watermarks or generic period drama imagery.
- Breath fog, candlelight glow, and frost patterns are recurring visual motifs.

## Character Bible

### @ELARA_FINCH — Elara Finch
- **Age / role:** 23; A bookbinder's daughter summoned to catalog a Duke's library; protagonist.
- **Physical description:** A slender English woman with pale ivory skin dusted with freckles across her nose, large hazel eyes that shift between green and gold, auburn hair pinned in a messy coil, and ink permanently staining her fingertips.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** forest-green wool dress with cream linen apron, leather bookbinder's gloves tucked at her belt, and worn ankle boots.
- **Signature object:** a small brass thimble on a chain around her neck.
- **Personality:** Intellectually fearless, emotionally guarded, stubbornly honest.
- **One-line arc:** She moves from survival-driven obedience to choosing possession as her own form of freedom.

### @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN — Duke Silas Blackthorn
- **Age / role:** 32; A reclusive widowed Duke with a reputation for darkness; male lead.
- **Physical description:** A tall, broad-shouldered Englishman with sharp aristocratic features, dark charcoal eyes that miss nothing, black hair slightly too long, a scar cutting through his left eyebrow, and an imposing stillness that fills every room.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** black wool coat with silver buttons, ivory cravat, dark grey waistcoat, black leather gloves, and a signet ring bearing the Blackthorn crest.
- **Signature object:** the Blackthorn signet ring — always visible on his right hand.
- **Personality:** Controlled, obsessive, devastatingly intelligent, capable of tenderness he would never name.
- **One-line arc:** He learns that claiming someone completely means letting them choose to stay.

### @MRS_VARMA — Mrs. Varma
- **Age / role:** 58; The Duke's housekeeper; keeper of the household's secrets.
- **Physical description:** A composed woman with dark olive skin, silver-streaked black hair pulled tight, knowing eyes and a mouth that reveals nothing.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** black silk dress with high collar, white apron with lace trim, and a heavy iron key ring at her waist.
- **Signature object:** iron house key on a black ribbon.
- **Personality:** Fiercely loyal to the Duke, protective of the house, suspicious of outsiders.
- **One-line arc:** She transitions from guard dog to reluctant witness to the Duke's transformation.

### @JULIAN_FOX — Julian Fox
- **Age / role:** 30; The Duke's charming, predatory cousin; antagonist.
- **Physical description:** A handsome man with sandy blond hair, ice-blue eyes, a lazy smile, and an elegance that never quite conceals the cruelty beneath.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** burgundy velvet coat with gold embroidery, cream silk shirt, tan riding breeches, and polished brown boots.
- **Signature object:** a gold-tipped riding crop carried idly.
- **Personality:** Witty, manipulative, entitled, sees people as playthings.
- **One-line arc:** His attempt to steal the Duke's inheritance and his bride exposes his own emptiness.

### @LADY_MARGARET — Lady Margaret
- **Age / role:** (Deceased, appeared 25 in portraits); The Duke's first wife; a ghost in the house.
- **Physical description:** Seen only in portraits and memories — a beautiful woman with raven-black hair, porcelain skin, and eyes that hold a warning.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe (portraits):** white satin gown with pearl choker, dark emerald pendant, hair arranged in classical style.
- **Signature object:** emerald pendant (recurring symbol).
- **Personality (in memory):** Seemingly gentle, secretly trapped, ultimately destroyed.
- **One-line arc:** Her story is the caution tale that Elara must survive.

### @THOMAS_FINCH — Thomas Finch
- **Age / role:** 52; Elara's father, a ruined bookbinder drowning in debt.
- **Physical description:** A thin, stooped man with grey-streaked auburn hair (like his daughter's), trembling hands, ink-calloused fingers, and the haunted eyes of a man who failed his family.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** threadbare brown wool coat, stained linen shirt, patched trousers, and a leather apron too worn to protect anything.
- **Signature object:** a cracked bookbinding press that he refuses to sell.
- **Personality:** Gentle, broken, full of love but unable to provide.
- **One-line arc:** His acceptance of the Duke's contract frees Elara and destroys him with guilt.

### @LUCY — Lucy
- **Age / role:** 19; A maid at Blackthorn Hall; Elara's unexpected ally.
- **Physical description:** A cheerful, round-faced young woman with rosy cheeks, auburn-brown hair in twin braids, and calloused hands from scrubbing floors.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** grey wool maid's dress with white cap and apron, and a small locket at her throat.
- **Signature object:** a tin of stolen candles she shares with Elara.
- **Personality:** Warm, brave, perceptive, loyal once trust is earned.
- **One-line arc:** She risks her position to warn Elara and finds her own voice.

### @FATHER_BENEDICT — Father Benedict
- **Age / role:** 65; The village vicar; moral conscience of the story.
- **Physical description:** A tall, thin man with kind weathered features, a white beard, watery blue eyes, and a slight stoop from years of pastoral work.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** black cassock with white clerical collar, a heavy wool scarf, and worn leather shoes.
- **Signature object:** a small leather-bound prayer book.
- **Personality:** Gentle, principled, unafraid to speak uncomfortable truths.
- **One-line arc:** He performs the marriage that transforms a contract into something real.

## Screenplay

### ACT I — INHERITANCE

## S01 — The Debt That Breathes
**Location:** `FINCH_BINDERY` · **Time:** EVENING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @THOMAS_FINCH

INT. FINCH BINDERY — EVENING

A cramped workshop lit by a single tallow candle. THOMAS FINCH hunches over his cracked binding press, hands shaking too badly to thread a needle. ELARA FINCH works beside him, stitching a Bible's spine with ink-stained precision. Creditor notices paper the walls like autumn leaves. Thomas coughs. Elara does not look up. She has learned that looking up means seeing the walls.

ELARA
We can sell the Chaucer. It has the brass clasps.

THOMAS
Your mother loved that one.

ELARA
Then it is worth more alive than dead.

He sets down his needle. His hands are worse than yesterday.

## S02 — The Summons Arrives
**Location:** `FINCH_BINDERY` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @THOMAS_FINCH

INT. FINCH BINDERY — MORNING

A black-sealed letter slides under the door. Elara's father cannot rise from his cot to retrieve it. She breaks the wax — the Blackthorn crest: a thorned rose. The letter commands the daughter of Thomas Finch to present herself at Blackthorn Hall to catalog the late Duchess's library. Payment: enough to clear the family debt. Her father's eyes widen with recognition — and terror.

ELARA
He knows your name.

THOMAS
Not mine.

ELARA
Then whose?

## S03 — The Road to Blackthorn
**Location:** `WINTER_ROAD` · **Time:** DAY · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH

EXT. WINTER ROAD — DAY

Elara walks alone along a frost-bitten road, her leather satchel of binding tools slung across her body. The hedgerows are skeletal with ice. Breath comes in small white clouds. She passes a milestone carved with a thorned rose and knows she is close. The landscape drains of color until only the grey sky and white frost remain.

ELARA
Every story about that house ends the same way. He did it. She vanished. The river took her. The winter took her. No one agrees on how. They all agree it happened.

She pulls her cloak tighter and does not turn back.

## S04 — The Gates of Blackthorn Hall
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_GATES` · **Time:** DUSK · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @MRS_VARMA

EXT. BLACKTHORN GATES — DUSK

Iron gates taller than a man, twisted into thorned vines, stand before a gravel drive leading to a silhouette of dark stone. MRS VARMA waits in the gateway like a sentinel. She does not smile. She does not welcome. She measures Elara with one look and turns to lead her inside.

MRS VARMA
You will sleep in the east wing. Do not use the upper corridor. The Duke does not receive callers. He receives people who answer letters.

ELARA
What is the difference?

Mrs. Varma does not answer. The gates close behind them.

## S05 — The First Glimpse of Him
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_FOYER` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @MRS_VARMA

INT. BLACKTHORN FOYER — NIGHT

A cavernous entrance hall lit by candelabras that throw long shadows on damask walls. Elara removes her gloves, suddenly conscious of her ink-stained hands. At the top of the staircase, barely visible in the upper gallery's half-dark, stands DUKE SILAS BLACKTHORN. He does not descend. He does not speak. He watches her the way one watches a door left ajar — patient, intent, as if he has been waiting for a very long time.

He turns and disappears without a word.

ELARA
(whispered)
He was not surprised to see me.

### ACT II — OBSESSION

## S06 — The Library That Remembered
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @MRS_VARMA

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — MORNING

Two stories of shelves bowing under centuries of leather and gold leaf. Dust motes hang motionless in slanting winter light. A fireplace large enough to stand in holds cold ashes. Elara sets down her satchel and exhales — the first full breath she has taken since arriving. This room is alive with knowledge. She touches a spine and feels something like recognition.

MRS VARMA
She arranged it by feeling, not subject. The late Duchess. You will disturb nothing until you understand her order.

ELARA
How long did that take you?

MRS VARMA
Ten years. I never did.

## S07 — The Journals with Her Name
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — AFTERNOON

Elara catalogues leather-bound journals, their spines cracked with age. She opens one dated thirteen years ago. Halfway through, she finds her own name — ELARA FINCH — written in elegant copperplate in a margin note beside a passage on the binding of psalters. The date is ten years before she was born. Her fingers tremble. She reads the entry again. The ink is old. The handwriting is deliberate. This was not a coincidence.

ELARA
(softly, to herself)
He knew. Before I existed. He wrote me down.

## S08 — The Duke Watches
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** EVENING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — EVENING

Candlelight. Elara works late, unaware of the figure standing in the doorway's shadow. The Duke watches her as she carefully lifts a page with a bone folder, as if she were performing surgery on something alive. He does not announce himself. She senses him the way animals sense a storm — in the air pressure, in the sudden stillness. When she turns, he is already gone. Only the scent of cold wool and smoke remains.

ELARA
He is always there. Just behind the edge of seeing.

## S09 — The Gallery of Dead Wives
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_GALLERY` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @LUCY

INT. BLACKTHORN GALLERY — NIGHT

A long corridor of portraits. LUCY, the young maid, shows Elara the house by stolen candlelight. They stop before a painting of LADY MARGARET — white satin gown, pearl choker, emerald pendant at her throat. The eyes are dark and knowing. Elara feels the portrait watching her back.

LUCY
They say he locked himself in here for a year after she died.
ELARA
How did she die?
LUCY
That is the question no one answers twice the same way.

Elara looks at her own reflection in the gilt frame — forest green dress, ink-stained fingers — and does not like the comparison.

## S10 — The First Touch
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** LATE NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — LATE NIGHT

Elara falls asleep at the reading desk, a book open against her cheek. The Duke enters silently. He stands over her, his shadow falling across the page. His gloved hand reaches down — not to shake her, not to wake her, but to brush a stray auburn curl from her forehead. His finger grazes her temple. She stirs. Their eyes meet across the smallest distance. Neither moves. The candle between them gutters.

His voice, when it comes, is barely audible.

DUKE SILAS
You are exactly as I wrote you.

### ACT III — THE BARGAIN

## S11 — The Contract on the Desk
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_STUDY` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN STUDY — MORNING

A desk of black oak. A single document. The Duke stands behind it, hands braced on the wood, signet ring catching the firelight. The contract reads: all Finch family debts cleared, father's workshop preserved, in exchange for Elara Finch serving as Duchess of Blackthorn for one winter — in name, in public, in residence. No conjugal obligation. No permanent arrangement. She may leave at spring thaw.

ELARA
Why me?

DUKE SILAS
Because you were always the answer. I simply had to wait for the question to arrive.

## S12 — The Fake Engagement
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_DINING_HALL` · **Time:** EVENING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @MRS_VARMA, @LUCY

INT. BLACKTHORN DINING HALL — EVENING

A long table set for two, though the house holds twenty. Mrs. Varma stands behind Elara's chair, adjusting her collar with proprietary hands. Lucy peeks from the serving doorway, wide-eyed. The Duke takes his seat and does not look at Elara until she is settled, then holds her gaze with an intensity that makes the candlelight seem dim.

DUKE SILAS
You will call me Silas in private. In public, you will call me Your Grace and allow no one to see you smile first.

ELARA
And if I smile first?

DUKE SILAS
Then I will spend the evening making it worth the scandal.

## S13 — The Jealousy at the Hunt Ball
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_BALLROOM` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @JULIAN_FOX

INT. BLACKTHORN BALLROOM — NIGHT

A hunt ball in full swing — candlelit, crystalline, the local gentry glittering in jewels and pretense. JULIAN FOX appears in burgundy velvet, claiming Elara for a dance with the ease of a man who has never been refused. The Duke watches from across the room, a glass of brandy untouched in his hand. When Julian's hand settles at Elara's waist, the Duke's knuckles whiten on the glass.

JULIAN
They say my cousin chose a bookbinder. I say he finally found someone who can read his moods.

ELARA
Your cousin's moods are not a difficult text.

JULIAN
Aren't they? Then why are you trembling?

The Duke crosses the room. He does not dance. He takes Elara's hand from Julian's waist and places it on his own coat.

## S14 — The Secret of the First Wife
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_STUDY` · **Time:** LATE NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN STUDY — LATE NIGHT

Elara has found a locked drawer and forced it open with a bone folder. Inside: letters from Lady Margaret's brother, describing his sister's slow poisoning, and a doctor's note that the Duke refused an autopsy. She confronts him. He does not deny the letters. He says they are incomplete.

ELARA
Did you kill her?

DUKE SILAS
She asked me to let her go. I held on too tightly. That is the closest thing to murder I have committed.

ELARA
And me?

A long silence. The fire pops.

DUKE SILAS
You are the only thing I have ever been afraid to hold.

## S15 — The Agreement
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** DAWN · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — DAWN

Winter light turns the library gold. Elara stands by the window, the contract in her hands. The Duke waits by the fireplace, still as a portrait. She has read every clause. She has found the escape clause: she may leave at spring, with her father's debts cleared, no conditions. She folds the document slowly.

ELARA
I will stay the winter. Not because of the debt. Because I need to know if what you wrote in those journals is true — or if I am just the woman who looked enough like a ghost to fill the space.

He crosses the room and stops exactly one arm's length from her.

DUKE SILAS
You were never a ghost. You were the only living thing I could imagine in this house.

### ACT IV — RUIN

## S16 — The Betrayal
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_STUDY` · **Time:** AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @JULIAN_FOX, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN STUDY — AFTERNOON

Julian has found Elara alone. He reveals that the Duke's journals contain entries not about love — but about ownership. He has been watching Elara since she was a child, arranging circumstances to bring her here. Julian shows her a page: the Duke purchased her father's debt from the original creditor three years ago. She was never summoned — she was harvested.

JULIAN
You are not a bride, my dear. You are a collection.

The Duke enters. He does not deny it.

## S17 — The Fire in the East Wing
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_EAST_WING` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @LUCY, @MRS_VARMA

INT. BLACKTHORN EAST WING — NIGHT

Elara's room. She has packed her satchel. The smell of smoke seeps under the door. The east wing corridor fills with orange light. Lucy screams from the stairwell. Mrs. Varma appears, pressing a key into Elara's hand — a servants' exit.

MRS VARMA
Go. Now. Do not look for him.

LUCY
The fire — it started in the gallery! The portraits —

Elara runs through smoke, the key cold in her palm, and does not look at the burning gallery where Lady Margaret's portrait turns to ash.

## S18 — She Tries to Flee
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_GROUNDS` · **Time:** PRE-DAWN · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH

EXT. BLACKTHORN GROUNDS — PRE-DAWN

Elara stumbles through snow beyond the grounds, her green dress stained with soot, her brass thimble swinging against her collarbone. The frost bites through her boots. She has gone perhaps a mile when she realizes she has been walking in a circle — the frost shows her own footprints ahead of her. The grounds of Blackthorn are larger than she understood. Or something is keeping her contained. She stops. Her breath comes in white clouds. She is alone. She is lost. She is furious.

ELARA
I will not be the ghost. I will not be the collection. I will not be the thing he wrote.

## S19 — He Lets Her Go
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_GATES` · **Time:** DAWN · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

EXT. BLACKTHORN GATES — DAWN

The iron gates. Elara reaches them at first light, exhausted, smoke-stained. The Duke stands on the other side. He has a horse saddled, a traveling cloak folded over its back, a letter of transit in his hand. He has not come to stop her. He has come to arm her.

DUKE SILAS
I bought the debt because I could not bear the thought of you starving while I did nothing. I did not arrange you. I waited for you. There is a difference and I know it does not excuse anything.

He opens the gate.

DUKE SILAS
Go. If you return, it must be because you chose to. Not because you had nowhere else.

## S20 — The Distance Between
**Location:** `VILLAGE_INN` · **Time:** DAY · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @FATHER_BENEDICT

INT. VILLAGE INN — DAY

A village inn. Elara sits by a fire, wrapped in the traveling cloak the Duke gave her. FATHER BENEDICT brings her tea and sits without invitation. He has known the Duke for decades. He has known grief.

FATHER BENEDICT
He came to me after Margaret died and said, 'Tell me how to stop loving what I cannot save.' I told him there is no stopping. Only choosing.

ELARA
What did he choose?

FATHER BENEDICT
He chose to wait. For ten years, he chose to wait. That is not obsession, child. That is the only patience I have ever seen that deserved to be called love.

### ACT V — DEVOTION

## S21 — The Return on Her Own Terms
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_GATES` · **Time:** DUSK · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @MRS_VARMA

EXT. BLACKTHORN GATES — DUSK

Elara returns to the gates. Not in the cloak he gave her — she has returned it. She wears her own forest-green dress, clean but worn, her satchel over her shoulder. She carries no contract. She carries nothing except herself. Mrs. Varma opens the gate without being asked. The Duke stands in the gravel drive, uncovered by hat or hood, snow catching in his too-long black hair. He has been waiting. He has always been waiting.

ELARA
I did not come back for the contract.

DUKE SILAS
I know.

ELARA
I came back because the alternative was a lifetime of listening for your footsteps and pretending I did not miss them.

## S22 — The Consummation
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_CHAMBERS` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN CHAMBERS — NIGHT

The Duke's private chambers — high windows black with winter night, a fire low and amber. Elara stands before him. No contract between them now. No audience. No bargain. He removes his gloves slowly, finger by finger. His bare hands take her face as if she were a text he had been trying to read for a decade and had only now learned the language.

DUKE SILAS
Say my name.

ELARA
Silas.

He closes his eyes. It is the first time he has heard his own name spoken like a prayer.

## S23 — The Public Claiming
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_BALLROOM` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @JULIAN_FOX, @MRS_VARMA, @LUCY

INT. BLACKTHORN BALLROOM — NIGHT

A second ball — this one Elara's choice. She enters on the Duke's arm, not as contract bride but as chosen Duchess. Julian watches from the crowd, his smile sharp enough to cut. Mrs. Varma stands at the door, and for the first time, she smiles. Lucy catches Elara's eye from behind the refreshment table and mouths: finally. The Duke leads Elara to the center of the floor and does not let go.

DUKE SILAS
(quietly, while dancing)
Let them stare. Let them write it down. Let Julian tell every version he likes. They will never be able to say I did not ask.

ELARA
You asked every day. I simply could not hear it until I was ready to answer.

## S24 — The Marriage
**Location:** `VILLAGE_CHURCH` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN, @FATHER_BENEDICT, @LUCY, @MRS_VARMA, @THOMAS_FINCH

INT. VILLAGE CHURCH — MORNING

A small village church, snow on the windowsills. Not a cathedral performance but a true thing. Thomas Finch walks his daughter down the aisle, his hands steadier than they have been in years. Lucy holds the flowers. Mrs. Varma stands as witness for the house. Father Benedict speaks the words with a voice that cracks on the word join. The Duke's signet ring is warm against Elara's finger as he slides it on — beside her brass thimble chain, beside the ink that will never wash out.

ELARA
I do.

DUKE SILAS
I always have.

## S25 — The True Duchess
**Location:** `BLACKTHORN_LIBRARY` · **Time:** WINTER MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @ELARA_FINCH, @DUKE_SILAS_BLACKTHORN

INT. BLACKTHORN LIBRARY — WINTER MORNING

Years later. The same library, but alive now — curtains drawn back, fire lit, books opened and scattered. Elara sits in the reading chair with a child balanced on her knee, pointing at gold lettering on a page. The Duke stands behind her, his hand on the chair's back, his signet ring catching light that falls through winter glass. The room smells of old paper and beeswax and woodsmoke. The thorned rose crest is carved into the chair's armrest, but now it looks less like a warning and more like a welcome.

ELARA
(reading aloud to the child)
And the Duke kept his word. Every word. Every winter. Every one.

DUKE SILAS
Not every word. I told you once that I would survive without you.

ELARA
That was the only lie you ever told.

The child laughs. The fire crackles. FADE OUT.
