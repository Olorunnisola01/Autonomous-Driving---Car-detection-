# THE RIVER REMEMBERS

*A Ghanaian magical-realism family drama*

## Production overview

**Format:** Feature screenplay / 20-scene storyboard
**Genre & tone:** Contemporary Ghanaian magical-realism drama; an intimate civic mystery that grows into a warm, hopeful community thriller.
**Setting:** Afenyo, a fictional lagoon town on Ghana's coast; contemporary day.
**Logline:** When a guarded solar engineer returns to her Ghanaian lagoon hometown and finds a resort project has darkened its public beacon, she, her lantern-maker grandmother and a teenage radio reporter must turn a hidden civic archive into a town-wide signal before the water is fenced away.
**Theme:** Public things survive when ordinary people decide to remember and care for them together.

## Visual style guide

Grounded cinematic West African magical realism; contemporary Ghanaian coastal life; human-scale performances; saturated indigo, lagoon teal, clay red and warm amber palette; natural skin texture; tactile fabric and weathered wood; 2.39:1 widescreen composition; subtle film grain; expressive realistic lighting. No written words, no logos, no watermark, no distorted hands, no duplicate people.

### Continuity rules

- Keep each principal in the exact signature wardrobe and signature object specified below in every present-day frame.
- Reuse each location registry description **verbatim** in image prompts.
- The magical-realism element is restrained: the lanterns and water carry emotional resonance, not overt supernatural effects.
- Favour community-scale images, real working hands, tactile blue glass, reed, copper, wood and water.
- Maintain 2.39:1 compositions and avoid readable text, logos, watermarks or generic tourism imagery.

## Character Bible

### @KOSI_MENSAH — Kosi Mensah
- **Age / role:** 29; A solar-field engineer returning to the lagoon town where she was raised; protagonist.
- **Physical description:** A tall, athletic Ghanaian woman with deep brown skin, alert dark eyes, a heart-shaped face and closely cropped natural coils.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** indigo utility jumpsuit with rolled sleeves, sand-coloured work boots and a narrow woven kente belt.
- **Signature object:** small gold Adinkra-disc earrings.
- **Personality:** Methodical, dryly funny and reluctant to depend on anybody.
- **One-line arc:** She trades self-protective competence for the courage to let a whole town carry the light with her.

### @AMA_ADJOVI — Ama Adjovi
- **Age / role:** 68; Kosi's grandmother, a retired lantern-maker and keeper of the town's oral records.
- **Physical description:** A compact elder with rich dark skin, silver hair woven into a low crown braid, knowing eyes and fine scar lines at her temples.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** deep navy kaba blouse, hand-dyed indigo wrapper with tiny white wave motifs and flat leather sandals.
- **Signature object:** three-strand amber bead necklace.
- **Personality:** Precise, playful and unmovable when a promise is at stake.
- **One-line arc:** She stops guarding the past alone and places its proof in the hands of the next generation.

### @SENA_ADJOVI — Sena Adjovi
- **Age / role:** 16; Ama's sharp-tongued grandniece and an aspiring community-radio reporter.
- **Physical description:** A slim Ghanaian teenager with warm umber skin, a round face, bright observant eyes and short copper-tipped twists.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** mustard oversized hoodie, blue-and-white printed ankle skirt and worn white canvas trainers.
- **Signature object:** turquoise over-ear radio headphones.
- **Personality:** Curious, fearless and allergic to adults editing the truth.
- **One-line arc:** She learns that reporting is not only recording a crisis but inviting people to speak for themselves.

### @KOJO_DANSO — Kojo Danso
- **Age / role:** 33; A lagoon-boat captain and Kosi's childhood friend.
- **Physical description:** A lean Ghanaian man with mahogany-brown skin, shoulder-length tidy locs, a soft smile and a small notch through his left eyebrow.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** faded forest-green canvas jacket over a blue-and-cream striped shirt, charcoal trousers and rubber deck boots.
- **Signature object:** weathered brass compass on a cord.
- **Personality:** Patient, teasing and quietly brave when water turns dangerous.
- **One-line arc:** He moves from watching the town endure to steering its resistance into action.

### @ESI_TETTEH — Esi Tetteh
- **Age / role:** 45; Leader of the fish-smokers' cooperative and the town's clearest public voice.
- **Physical description:** A broad-shouldered Ghanaian woman with dark skin, a powerful stance, close-cropped hair and a crescent scar above one eyebrow.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** brick-red wax-print dress beneath a soot-black oilskin apron, patterned headwrap and sturdy black sandals.
- **Signature object:** smooth charred-wood smoking paddle.
- **Personality:** Blunt, generous and impatient with polite evasion.
- **One-line arc:** Her righteous anger becomes disciplined collective leadership rather than a solitary fight.

### @KWAME_AGYEMAN — Kwame Agyeman
- **Age / role:** 36; The polished local liaison for the resort project and Kosi's former university friend.
- **Physical description:** A slender Ghanaian man with medium-brown skin, neat low-cropped hair, a long thoughtful face and rimless spectacles.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** white linen shirt under a caramel lightweight field jacket, dark tailored trousers and brown loafers.
- **Signature object:** antique bronze lapel pin shaped like a compass rose.
- **Personality:** Diplomatic, ambitious and deeply uncomfortable with his own compromises.
- **One-line arc:** He stops confusing access to power with service and risks his position to release the truth.

### @NII_LAMPTEY — Nii Lamptey
- **Age / role:** 55; District Assembly Chair whose partnership with the resort threatens Afenyo's public lagoon access; antagonist.
- **Physical description:** A tall, imposing Ghanaian man with deep espresso skin, a shaved head, heavy-lidded eyes and a neatly trimmed grey goatee.
- **Fixed signature wardrobe:** tailored charcoal caftan with a subtle black geometric weave, polished black shoes and a silver wristwatch.
- **Signature object:** black ebony walking cane with a carved heron handle.
- **Personality:** Controlled, eloquent and convinced that progress must have winners.
- **One-line arc:** Public exposure forces him to confront that authority without consent is only a well-dressed form of theft.

## Screenplay

### ACT I — RETURN

## S01 — The Roof That Hums
**Location:** `ACCRA_SOLAR_ROOF` · **Time:** DAY · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH

EXT. ACCRA SOLAR ROOF — DAY

KOSI MENSah moves across a roof with a tester in one hand and a socket wrench in the other. A row of panels starts humming after her repair. Her phone vibrates: a voice message from AMa says, "The lagoon is being measured again. Come before they turn our water into a gate." Kosi watches the city shimmer, deletes a work callback, and packs her tools.

KOSI
I said I was done coming home for emergencies.

She says it to the empty roof, then pockets the phone anyway.

## S02 — Road to Afenyo
**Location:** `AFENYO_BUS_STOP` · **Time:** LATE AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @SENA_ADJOVI

EXT. AFENYO BUS STOP — LATE AFTERNOON

A tro-tro coughs away, leaving Kosi with her tool case. SENA pushes through cassava sellers wearing headphones and records a cheery arrival report. She announces Kosi's return into a dead microphone, then admits that the transmitter works only when the sun does.

SENA
Grandma said not to tell you she was scared. So I am telling you.

KOSI
That is not how secrets work.

SENA
Then Afenyo needs better secrets.

## S03 — A Lantern Left Dark
**Location:** `COMPOUND_COURTYARD` · **Time:** SUNSET · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @SENA_ADJOVI

EXT. COMPOUND COURTYARD — SUNSET

AMA has laid out Kosi's favourite pepper soup but has not lit the courtyard lantern. Kosi hugs her, noticing the tremor in Ama's hands. Sena hangs a string of blue glass scraps in the mango tree. Ama says the town once lit every channel lantern before the first rain; now even the beacon has gone black.

AMA
A dark lantern is not only darkness. It tells boats they are not expected.

Kosi studies the unlit frame. The sentence lands harder than the welcome.

## S04 — Price of the Water
**Location:** `AFENYO_MARKET` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @SENA_ADJOVI, @ESI_TETTEH, @KOJO_DANSO

EXT. AFENYO MARKET — MORNING

ESI slaps a wet survey notice onto a fish tray: a new resort will fence the public landing. KOJO says the water has smelled like metal since the drilling began. Kosi checks the notice's power diagrams and spots a line running toward the navigation buoy. Sena records every complaint.

ESI
They call it access improvement. Whose feet are improved by a locked gate?

KOSI
Show me where they put the cables.

## S05 — The Polite Hearing
**Location:** `DISTRICT_ASSEMBLY_HALL` · **Time:** AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @ESI_TETTEH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN, @NII_LAMPTEY, @SENA_ADJOVI

INT. DISTRICT ASSEMBLY HALL — AFTERNOON

NII introduces the resort as a future of jobs and clean power. KWAME, immaculate and surprised to see Kosi, explains the environmental safeguards. Esi demands a public copy of the lagoon agreement. Nii promises one after the vote. Kosi recognizes the answer as a lock.

KOSI
If the plan is safe, why is the map not on the wall?

NII
Engineer Mensah, trust is also infrastructure.

Sena quietly starts recording.

## S06 — The Black Buoy
**Location:** `LAGOON_CHANNEL` · **Time:** DUSK · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @KOJO_DANSO

EXT. LAGOON CHANNEL — DUSK

Kojo steers a canoe toward the dark buoy. Kosi tests the housing; its solar battery has been bypassed by a new buried cable. A thin slick shivers through the water. Kojo points to resort lights burning far away.

KOJO
The channel has always carried us out.

KOSI
Then someone has taught it to carry their dirt in.

She photographs the severed connection and decides not to leave.

### ACT II — THE SIGNAL

## S07 — The Ledger in the Lantern
**Location:** `AMA_LANTERN_WORKSHOP` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @SENA_ADJOVI

INT. AMA LANTERN WORKSHOP — NIGHT

Kosi repairs a lantern while Ama watches. A loose blue pane reveals a rolled, water-stained ledger page underneath. It lists public landing rights and an old beacon frequency in Ama's handwriting. Sena sees a code in the wave marks.

AMA
The old people hid papers where officials never looked: inside what kept us safe.

KOSI
This could prove the shore belongs to the town.

Ama closes Kosi's hand around the page.

## S08 — Open Frequency
**Location:** `SENA_RADIO_SHACK` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @SENA_ADJOVI, @KOSI_MENSAH, @ESI_TETTEH

INT. SENA RADIO SHACK — MORNING

Sena powers the station from Kosi's temporary battery. Esi gives the first on-air testimony about losing the landing. Calls come in from boat crews and market women. Kosi asks Sena not to name anyone until they have proof.

SENA
The proof is that people are afraid to say it.

KOSI
Fear is evidence. It is not enough evidence.

Sena puts the microphone between them both.

## S09 — Map with a Missing Line
**Location:** `RESORT_FENCE` · **Time:** AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN

EXT. RESORT FENCE — AFTERNOON

Kwame meets Kosi at a chained gate, carrying an approved site plan. A utility route disappears beneath a black redaction bar. He says the contractor will not show him the discharge run either. Kosi calls that a choice.

KWAME
I took this job to get a seat at the table.

KOSI
Then stop pretending the table is not sitting on our water.

Before leaving, he lets her photograph a survey number on the plan.

## S10 — Under the Roots
**Location:** `MANGROVE_PASSAGE` · **Time:** LATE AFTERNOON · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @KOJO_DANSO, @SENA_ADJOVI

EXT. MANGROVE PASSAGE — LATE AFTERNOON

Kojo poles a narrow canoe through roots while Kosi trails a sensor on a wire. Sena films in silence. The meter spikes near a concealed pipe; warm grey water pulses out. A construction engine stops abruptly in the distance.

SENA
If they see us?

KOJO
Then you keep your camera still.

Kosi seals a sample and sends Sena ahead with the footage.

## S11 — King Tide
**Location:** `FLOODED_COMPOUND` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KOJO_DANSO

EXT. FLOODED COMPOUND — NIGHT

A king tide pours into Ama's courtyard. Kosi lifts electrical leads above the water as Kojo wades in with a canoe. Ama refuses to abandon the lantern crate until Sena carries it. The family floats their tools to safety.

AMA
The water remembers every blocked path.

KOSI
Then we will make it testify.

A blue lantern flickers to life in the storm.

## S12 — Night of the Red Moon
**Location:** `OLD_BEACON_HUT` · **Time:** PRE-DAWN · **Speaking characters:** @AMA_ADJOVI, @KOSI_MENSAH, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KOJO_DANSO

INT. OLD BEACON HUT — PRE-DAWN

Ama leads them to a forgotten beacon hut. She tells how the town's elders saved the landing deed during a colonial survey by encoding its archive number in a lantern rhythm. Kosi recognizes the rhythm as the frequency in the hidden page.

AMA
Memory is not a box. It is a signal someone must answer.

Sena turns the phrase into a radio sign-off.

## S13 — The Yard Stands Still
**Location:** `FISH_SMOKE_YARD` · **Time:** DAY · **Speaking characters:** @ESI_TETTEH, @KOSI_MENSAH, @SENA_ADJOVI, @NII_LAMPTEY

EXT. FISH-SMOKE YARD — DAY

Esi calls a one-day work stoppage. Fish trays are stacked across the road to the resort gate, but no one blocks ambulances or families. Nii arrives to call the action unlawful. Kosi offers water-sample results; he refuses to read them.

ESI
We are not stopping work. We are stopping theft from wearing a hard hat.

NII
You are making a spectacle.

SENA
No, Chairman. We are broadcasting one.

## S14 — Archive of the Unheard
**Location:** `COUNCIL_ARCHIVE` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN, @SENA_ADJOVI

INT. COUNCIL ARCHIVE — NIGHT

Kwame opens the archive after hours. They find the original landing record, plus a recent amendment bearing a digital stamp from an office that was closed that day. Sena photographs every page. A sound in the stairwell makes Kwame freeze.

KWAME
If I send this out, I am finished here.

KOSI
If you hide it, so are we.

He hands her the keycard.

## S15 — When the Power Fails
**Location:** `LAGOON_PROMENADE` · **Time:** DUSK · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @ESI_TETTEH, @KOJO_DANSO, @SENA_ADJOVI

EXT. LAGOON PROMENADE — DUSK

The town's power fails during the promenade meeting. Ama collapses briefly from exhaustion, not injury; Kosi steadies her. Around them, people raise phone lights, then hand lanterns. Kosi realizes the old beacon can transmit the archive frequency farther than the radio shack.

KOSI
Tomorrow the town does not ask for a hearing. The town gives one.

ESI
And who will light it?

KOSI
All of us.

### ACT III — PUBLIC LIGHT

## S16 — A Thousand Small Lights
**Location:** `FESTIVAL_SQUARE` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KOJO_DANSO, @ESI_TETTEH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN

EXT. FESTIVAL SQUARE — MORNING

Afenyo makes lanterns from blue glass, jars and recycled solar cells. Kosi teaches children to wire safe low-voltage lights. Esi coordinates food. Kwame arrives without his lapel pin and offers the master access code. Ama gives Sena the oldest lantern.

AMA
A light means nothing if it only points at itself.

SENA
Good. Mine points at everybody.

## S17 — Signal on the Water
**Location:** `LAGOON_BEACON` · **Time:** BLUE HOUR · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @KOJO_DANSO, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KWAME_AGYEMAN

EXT. LAGOON BEACON — BLUE HOUR

Kojo brings Kosi, Sena and Kwame to the beacon as a flotilla of lantern canoes forms behind them. Kosi connects a repaired solar cell. Sena broadcasts the archive scans and water tests. Kwame sends the unredacted map to regional reporters. The beacon answers with a steady blue pulse.

SENA (ON AIR)
Afenyo is speaking. Please do not call this noise.

The whole lagoon hears the signal.

## S18 — The Chair Answers
**Location:** `COMMUNITY_HALL` · **Time:** NIGHT · **Speaking characters:** @NII_LAMPTEY, @KOSI_MENSAH, @ESI_TETTEH, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KWAME_AGYEMAN, @AMA_ADJOVI

INT. COMMUNITY HALL — NIGHT

The broadcast draws the town into the hall. The scanned deeds and live water readings appear on the blank sheet. Nii enters to reclaim the room, but one fisherman names the date the landing was promised and another woman corrects him. Nii finally sees that the record is no longer his to bury.

NII
Development requires difficult decisions.

AMA
Then let the people who carry the difficulty make them.

Nii lowers his cane.

## S19 — The Water Is Public
**Location:** `DISTRICT_ASSEMBLY_HALL` · **Time:** MORNING · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @ESI_TETTEH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN, @NII_LAMPTEY, @SENA_ADJOVI

INT. DISTRICT ASSEMBLY HALL — MORNING

Under public observation, the assembly suspends the resort permit pending an independent inquiry. Nii reads the order himself. Kwame submits his resignation and the complete project files. Esi insists the victory include a cooperative water board, not merely a pause.

KOSI
A pause is only a door if we decide what walks through it.

The room votes for the board. Sena catches the sound on her recorder.

## S20 — First Light, Shared
**Location:** `SUNRISE_PIER` · **Time:** SUNRISE · **Speaking characters:** @KOSI_MENSAH, @AMA_ADJOVI, @SENA_ADJOVI, @KOJO_DANSO, @ESI_TETTEH, @KWAME_AGYEMAN

EXT. SUNRISE PIER — SUNRISE

The repaired beacon flashes over the lagoon. Kosi and Kojo secure a community-owned solar battery at the pier while Esi's cooperative prepares boats. Ama watches Sena begin a new radio programme: The Water Remembers. Kwame, now in work gloves over the same clothes, joins the line carrying nets.

SENA (ON AIR)
This is Afenyo. The light is public. The water is public. Good morning.

Kosi looks at Ama. The elder nods. The lagoon brightens. FADE OUT.
