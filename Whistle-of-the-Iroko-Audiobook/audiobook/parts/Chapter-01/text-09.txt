My body is not the only thing that would go into that ground, Chidi." "Grandma—" "You remember the whistle? The old brass one? The one I used to blow when I called you in from the bush at dusk?" "Yes," I whispered. I remembered it the way you remember a smell — instantly, whole. Three long blasts, and the sound carried over the cassava fields, over the stream, and every child in the compound would scatter home like chickens before rain.
