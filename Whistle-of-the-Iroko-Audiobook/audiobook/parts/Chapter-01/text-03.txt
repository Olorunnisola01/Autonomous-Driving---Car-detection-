It was a crumpled bus ticket, folded into the wallet of a girl who had been rescued by the road near Lagos—Abeokuta before she could be taken out of the country in a container. On the back of the ticket, in handwriting that was not hers, was a phone number. I had called it from a prepaid SIM. A man had picked up on the second ring and said one word: "Gbakwa?" — hang up — and the line had gone dead. I knew that voice.
