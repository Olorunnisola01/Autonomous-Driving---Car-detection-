I had written about him in my third article. The man they called Odogwu. So when my phone buzzed at 3:47 a.m. and I saw MAMA NNENNA on the screen, I almost didn't answer. The only people who call at that hour are the dead and the dying, and people in trouble. My grandmother was seventy-eight years old, living alone in the family compound in Oda Village, Enugu State, three hundred miles and one lifetime away from Surulere.
